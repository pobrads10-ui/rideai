RideAi Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026. This policy covers the RideAi app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

The short version

  • RideAi has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your chats are saved only on your own device. Tap "New chat" to delete them.
  • To answer you, RideAi may send your question to Wikipedia, and on Android to Google's Gemini AI. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

What RideAi stores

Your messages and RideAi's answers are saved on your device so the chat is still there next time you open the app. They are not uploaded to us. "New chat" deletes them, and deleting the app removes everything.

Where your questions go

iPhone, iPad and Mac: answers come from Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. On devices without Apple Intelligence, RideAi answers simple questions itself (maths, the time and date) and looks facts up on Wikipedia.

Android: answers come from Gemini Nano on your phone when it has it. Otherwise your recent chat is sent to Google's Gemini service through Google Firebase to get an answer. Google's handling of that data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

Both: to give up-to-date answers, the topic of your question is sent to Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) to fetch a short summary.

Your location

Only if you allow it, RideAi uses your approximate location (your town or city) so it can answer questions like "where am I?" or "what's the weather like here?". The town or city name is sent with your question to the AI that answers it (see above). RideAi does not keep a history of where you've been, and you can turn location off at any time in your device's settings.

Games RideAi makes

When you ask RideAi to make a game, the AI writes the game and it is saved on your device so you can play it again. If you add a game to your home screen, it is still stored only on your device.

The microphone

RideAi only listens after you tap the microphone button, and stops when you stop talking. Your speech is turned into text by your device's speech recognition (Apple's on iPhone, iPad and Mac; Google's Speech Services on Android), which may send the audio to Apple or Google to be transcribed. RideAi does not keep recordings.

Children

RideAi is designed to give friendly, family-safe answers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. AI answers can be wrong, so younger children should use RideAi with a grown-up.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or problems? Please contact us on GitHub.
